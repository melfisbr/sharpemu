Target: PPSA01341 eboot SHA256 22DD832BAE21ABEB57FC66074D2BFA5F4C53F6EFED4544A9BE6B9E0D0316130E

Purpose:
Preserve the V74.0.25 WRITE_DATA synchronization repair and test a 10-host-lane
guest affinity topology on hosts with >=16 logical processors. Demon's Souls
creates guest workers for CPUs 0-12. The current source's own 16-lane benchmark
reported its best result with 6 reserved lanes, leaving 10 guest-usable lanes.
On a 24-thread host the ratio-based default leaves 15 usable lanes, so the
13 BPE workers are not packed at all. RUN_4 sets SHARPEMU_RESERVED_HOST_LANES
to ProcessorCount-10 (14 on a 24-thread host) without changing the default source.
