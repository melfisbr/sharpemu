V73.9 diagnostic correction

The V73.8 field guest_frames=1 was based on the INFO line
"Vulkan VideoOut presented guest frame". VulkanVideoPresenter emits this line
only while _firstGuestDrawPresented is false. It is a one-time milestone, not
a frame counter.

V73.9 enables SHARPEMU_LOG_VIDEOOUT_FPS=1. ReportPresentedFrame increments the
real _presentedFrameCount after successful vkQueuePresentKHR calls.
