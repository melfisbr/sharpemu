Conclusion:
APR/AMPR core argument decoding is not the missing UI implementation. The
source-level correctness defect found in the read chain is the unlocked
FileStream use in KernelReadUnderscore while KernelCloseCore disposes the same
stream under a monitor. V73.7 fixes this without changing APR/AMPR ABI.
