`if (_playback is not null || _frameBuffer is not null)`. One of them is the
queued-attach check. V31.3 tried to patch that generic condition with an
exact-once primitive and later tried to patch the queued occurrence again.

2. The queued-attach pass is idempotent and accepts the already-patched structure.
3. The generic `_playback is not null` completion poll is selected structurally:
   the last matching block before the existing `Bink2 bridge completed:` marker.
4. Post-patch validation rejects any remaining legacy active/busy condition.
5. RAD remains required; no NIHAV/FFmpeg fallback is introduced.
