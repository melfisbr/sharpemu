V74.0.67.2.12

After the primary BPE recovery successfully returned payload, a second call to
the same helper faulted.

The second caller uses R13 for the returned iterator and R15 as its end
sentinel. Its post-call sequence is verified by bytes, not by absolute address.

The recovery is extended only for:
  LastImportNid=GuchCTefuZw
  RDI==R13
  R15==payload
  exact helper/container/caller signatures

Return remains RAX=payload.
