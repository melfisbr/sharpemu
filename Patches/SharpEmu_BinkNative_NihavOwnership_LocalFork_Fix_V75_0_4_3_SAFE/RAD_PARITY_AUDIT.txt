Ownership invariant
-------------------
Normal runtime: one BK2 -> one NativeRad decoder -> one A/V clock.
NIHAV cannot create a parallel process/decoder for that BK2 while native exclusive ownership is active.

Important distinction: attract_movie.bk2 is video-only in the current Demon's Souls path; its existing AT9 sidecar remains SharpEmu-owned and is latched to the same first-visible-frame event.
Runtime execution must still be confirmed by RUN_4 and RUN_5 on Windows.
