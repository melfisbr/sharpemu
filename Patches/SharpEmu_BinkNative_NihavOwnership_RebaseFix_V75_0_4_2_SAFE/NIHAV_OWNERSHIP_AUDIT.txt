V75.0.4.2 NIHAV ownership review
=================================

Finding from V75.0.4 review:
- V75.0.4 changed HostMovieBridge routing, loops and completion handoffs.
- V75.0.4 did NOT modify NihavBink2Decoder.cs itself.
- Therefore a legacy/direct NIHAV call could still open nihav-tool even with NativeRad installed.

V75.0.4.1 functional correction, preserved/rebased by V75.0.4.2:
1. NihavBink2Decoder.cs contains SHARPEMU_BINK_NATIVE_NIHAV_OWNERSHIP_V75_0_4_1 (functional ownership marker retained by the V75.0.4.2 structural rebase).
2. NihavBink2Decoder.TryOpen refuses normal-runtime opens when NativeRad is available, preferred and exclusive.
3. HostMovieBridge promotes legacy UI branches that rewrite mode to NIHAV back to NativeRad before audio probing.
4. ResolveMode defaults to NativeRad when the adapter is available and exclusive ownership is enabled.
5. Demon's Souls boot policy sets SHARPEMU_BINK_NATIVE_EXCLUSIVE=1.
6. Test forces exclusive ownership and records any unexpected 'Bink2 NIHAV bridge attached' line.

NIHAV is not deleted: explicit SHARPEMU_BINK_MODE=nihav/bink2 or SHARPEMU_BINK_NATIVE_EXCLUSIVE=0 can still be used for diagnostics/compatibility.
