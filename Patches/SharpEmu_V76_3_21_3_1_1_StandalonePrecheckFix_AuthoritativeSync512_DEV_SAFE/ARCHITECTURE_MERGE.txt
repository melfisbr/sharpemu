ARCHITECTURE NOTE - V76.3.21.3.1.1
===================================
The first V76.3.21.3.1 package incorrectly made its PRECHECK depend on a V21.3
CLI environment assignment. That is exactly the assignment the authoritative
consumer patch was designed not to trust, so the prerequisite was circular.

This revision removes that circular prerequisite. PRECHECK validates the real
VulkanVideoPresenter weighted-closure field and its V21.2 branch structure.
APPLY then replaces that consumer with a PPSA01341-authoritative 512 budget.

The runtime semantics are otherwise unchanged: no FIFO bypass, no fake wait
satisfaction, no queue-family ownership change, no AGC packet semantic change.
