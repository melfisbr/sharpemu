ARCHITECTURE NOTE - V76.3.21.3.1
=================================
The previous V21.3 relied on process environment values written by the CLI policy.
The captured runtime proved that the weighted closure still instantiated with a
4096 sync budget. This revision removes that ordering ambiguity for PPSA01341 by
binding the conservative 512 budget at the VulkanVideoPresenter consumer itself.

This is deliberately narrower than a new scheduler. It does not bypass FIFO, does
not satisfy waits, does not change queue-family ownership and does not touch AGC
packet semantics. Diagnostic probes are enabled only by RUN_4 to determine whether
the next blocker is guest-thread progress or an active scene-pipeline gap.
