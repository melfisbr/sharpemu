Guest-media sequencing finding from V72.4.3.2.31.7.8

The host direct boot executed:
ps_studios_logo.bk2 -> logo_intro.bk2 -> logo_intro_loop.bk2
and reported bink2.direct_boot_completed.

After handoff, the guest naturally requested ps_studios_logo.bk2 again.
This shows that even the canonical host-forced intro chain can duplicate the guest's own startup state.

V74.0.22 therefore does not force any boot movie. Natural guest Bink requests own the sequence, while the completion shim only returns completion to the guest after a natural request.
